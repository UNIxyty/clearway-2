Open it from the portal's sidebar: **Ops Agent** › **History**. It lists your own chats with the agent, newest first.
Nobody else's chats are listed.

[screenshot: ops-agent-history | History: Your earlier chats with the agent, to find and open again.]

## Find an earlier chat
Find a chat by its title, a word in it, or a flight or airport it mentions.

1. Type in **Search threads, messages, callsigns…**. The count next to it says how many chats match, for example
   **1 of 5**.
2. To narrow the list, press a filter: **With changes**, **With files** or **Voice**. Press it again to remove it.
3. Read the list. Chats are grouped by **TODAY**, **YESTERDAY**, **LAST WEEK** and **OLDER**.

Each chat shows its title, its last message, the flights and airports it mentions, and how many messages, changes,
files and emails it has.

:::example A search
Search: `EVRA`
Result: the chats that mention EVRA
:::

:::how-it-works
The search looks in your 80 most recent chats.
:::

## Open an earlier chat
Open a chat to read it, or to go on asking.

1. Press the chat in the list. It opens on its own page.
2. Read it, or type a new question to go on.

From the panel beside a page, press the clock (**History**) at the top of the panel, then press the chat. It opens
in the panel. Press **All history in full page →** for this page.

Unverified: Opening a chat from the panel's History. Opening one from this page was checked.

:::note A chat cannot be renamed or deleted
There is no button for either. The title comes from the first question.
:::
