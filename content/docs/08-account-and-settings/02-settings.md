Open it from the portal's sidebar: **Settings**. It has three parts: **Appearance**, **Customisation** and
**Preferences**. Everything here is for you only, and is saved to your account as you change it.

## Choose light or dark
Roles: All roles
Choose how the portal looks on every device you sign in on.

1. Under **Appearance**, choose **Match this device**, **Light** or **Dark**. The portal changes at once.

The same choice is in the sun or moon button at the top right of the portal.

:::note The wall keeps its own colours
This is the one place to choose it. The Digital Wall's console pages (Flights, NOTAM Check and the rest) and the wall's
guide follow it, at once, even in a tab that is already open; they have no switch of their own. The big screen itself
does not: its colours are in the wall's own [Settings](digital-wall/settings). Press
**Wall display settings** to go there.
:::

## Pin pages or airports to the top bar
Roles: All roles
Keep up to 5 pages or airports beside the search box at the top.

1. Under **Pinned to the search bar**, press **Pin a page or airport**.
2. Type a page name, or an ICAO code, in **A page, or an ICAO like EVRA**.
3. Press the page or airport in the list. A message says it is pinned, and it shows in the top bar.
4. To change the order, drag a pin by its handle. Or press the handle, then the arrow keys.
5. To remove one, press its **×** button.

The count says how many you have, for example **2 of 5**. The pins show on wide screens only.

:::example Two pins
Airport: `EVRA`
Page: `Service status`
:::

## Choose the dashboard's blocks
Roles: All roles
Choose which blocks your dashboard shows.

1. Under **Dashboard blocks**, press **Customise dashboard**. The dashboard opens, ready to change.
2. Change the blocks, as in [Customise the dashboard](dashboard/the-dashboard#customise-the-dashboard).

## Set the table density
Roles: All roles
Show more rows on the screen.

1. Under **Table density**, choose **Comfortable** or **Compact**.

**Comfortable** rows are 52 pixels high, **Compact** rows 32. It applies to the portal's tables, for example
**Service status** and **Admin** › **Users**.

## Choose your start page
Roles: All roles
Choose the page you land on after signing in.

1. Under **Start page**, choose **Dashboard**, **Airport search**, **Service status**, **Ops Agent chat** or **Flight
   intake**.

A link to another page still opens that page.

## Reduce motion
Roles: All roles
Turn off animations, such as loading shimmer and sliding panels.

1. Under **Motion**, choose **Reduce**. To follow your device's setting instead, choose **Match device**.
