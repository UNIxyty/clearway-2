---

## AIP
Aeronautical Information Publication
A country's official book of its airports, airspace and procedures. The airport pages in the portal are built from it.
Example: `EVRA AD 2`
Where: **AIP & Documents** › **Airport search**.

## CAA
Civil Aviation Authority
A country's aviation regulator. On the wall, a CAA marker on a flight means its CAA details are still to be looked at.
Where: The **CAA** marker on a flight pill; **Digital Wall** › **CAA details** in the console.

## ICAO code
Four-letter airport code
The code air traffic control uses for an airport. It is not the three-letter code printed on tickets.
Example: `EVRA` (Riga), not `RIX`
Where: Everywhere an airport appears.

## IMP
Important
An item entered on the console's Important page, for the flights it applies to. The IMP marker shows on those flights until that part of the flight is checked.
Where: The **IMP** marker on a flight pill; **Digital Wall** › **Important** in the console.

## Leon
The operators' flight management system
Where the airlines and charter companies plan their flights. The wall reads each operator's flights from Leon; nothing on the wall changes Leon.
Where: **Digital Wall** › **Operators**.

## NOTAM
Notice to air missions
A short notice about something temporary at an airport or in its airspace: a closed taxiway, an unlit runway, a crane.
Example: `A1234/26`
Where: The NOTAM sign on the wall; **Digital Wall** › **NOTAM Check** in the console.

## NTM
NOTAMs, on a flight pill
The marker that says a flight's NOTAMs are still to be looked at.
Where: The **NTM** marker on a flight pill.

## Refresh token
The key that lets the wall read an operator's flights
A token the operator's Leon admin makes in Leon. It is stored encrypted and never shown again.
Where: **Digital Wall** › **Operators** › **Add operator** or **Edit operator**.

## UTC
Coordinated Universal Time
The one time zone aviation uses everywhere. Times written with a Z at the end are UTC.
Example: `14:20Z`
Where: The clocks along the top of the wall; flight times.

## Webhook
Leon's change notices
How Leon tells the wall about a change as it happens, instead of waiting for the next sync. They are tied to the operator's refresh token.
Where: **Digital Wall** › **Webhooks** in the console.

## WX
Weather
The marker that says a flight's weather is still to be looked at.
Where: The **WX** marker on a flight pill.
