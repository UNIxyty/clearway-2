## Ops staff
Run the shift: today's flights on the Digital Wall, the daily NOTAM check, and airport documents.
Uses: Digital Wall · AIP & Documents

## Admins
Approve accounts, decide what each role may do, and set up the operators whose flights the wall shows.
Uses: Admin · Digital Wall

## Developers
Answer the threads people send from Help & support, and decide who can use the Ops Agent.
Uses: Developer · Ops Agent
